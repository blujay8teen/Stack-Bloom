Stack Bloom Support

Stack Bloom is a block-stacking game for iPhone and iPad, published by Bradley Solutions.

Contact

For help, feedback or bug reports, email bradleyjayrussell@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

My purchase didn't unlock

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads or the Starter Bundle in the Shop. Banner and full-screen ads are turned off; rewarded ads stay available but only play when you choose to watch one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and select the purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress and gems are stored on your device. Remove Ads, the Theme Pack and the Starter Bundle can be restored after reinstalling; gem packs that have been used cannot.
