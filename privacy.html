Stack Bloom

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Stack Bloom on iPhone or iPad, who collects it, and what it is used for. Stack Bloom is published by Bradley Solutions ("we", "us").

Summary

  • Stack Bloom has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card or bank details.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested as suitable for children and are not personalised.
  • The app does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites.

Information linked to you

Name

If you contact us for support, we receive the name you give us or that is attached to your message. We use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of the request.

Payment information

In-app purchases (gems, the Theme Pack, Remove Ads and the Starter Bundle) are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. Apple collects and holds your payment details under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive confirmation of what was bought so the app can unlock it, and we may see purchase details if you send them to us with a support or refund question. We do not receive or store your card number or billing address.

Information not linked to you

Email address

If you email us, we receive your email address. We use it only to answer your message. We do not add it to a mailing list, combine it with gameplay or device data, or share it.

Diagnostics

Crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data (such as app launch times and ad loading errors) may be collected by Apple, if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers, and by the Google Mobile Ads software built into the app. This information is used to find and fix problems and is not tied to your name or contact details.

Advertising

Stack Bloom shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded ads supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google's software in the app may collect your device's IP address (which indicates a general location such as city or country), device and app identifiers, how you interact with ads, and the diagnostic data described above. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Every ad request from Stack Bloom is marked as child-directed and limited to general-audience content, which tells Google not to use the request for personalised advertising. Buying Remove Ads or the Starter Bundle turns off banner and full-screen ads; rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, gems, unlocked themes, mission progress and settings are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Children

Stack Bloom is suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. Purchases sit behind a "Grown-ups only" question, and ads are requested as child-directed. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Apple (purchases, diagnostics) and Google (advertising, diagnostics) as described above, or where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting information

Support messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet our legal obligations, then deleted. You can ask us at any time for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
